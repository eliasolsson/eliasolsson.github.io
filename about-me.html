About Me


▶ Get to know me!

I'm a Game Designer based in Uppsala, focused on level and content design. I studied Game Design at PlaygroundSquad between 2023 and 2025, where I primarily focused on Level Design and QA Testing, shown on two PlayStation 5 projects: Rocket Rider in Unreal Engine 5 and BEETLEBOT made on an in-house engine.
Since 2018 I've made content for The Road to 56, a Hearts of Iron IV mod with over 1,500,000 Steam subscribers. I script focus trees and seasonal events, and I help fix various bugs and issues reported by the community on the mods' Discord and subreddit. I started out writing missions for Team Fortress 2 community maps (something I still do occasionally) and assisted in running their playtests. I'm looking for work in level design, content design or QA, anywhere in Sweden or remote.
Feedback on my work is always welcome. You can reach me at eliasolsson.gamedev@gmail.com.
